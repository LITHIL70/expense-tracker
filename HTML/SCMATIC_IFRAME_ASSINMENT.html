1. Nepal Flood Disaster

Glacier collapse sparks deadly floods

A catastrophic flood in Nepal has killed more than 900 people and left 4,247 missing, including hundreds of hydropower workers trapped in tunnels. Rescue operations involve nearly 21,000 personnel, but debris and destroyed roads pose major challenges. The Red Cross estimates 90,000+ people affected, with China linking the disaster to climate change.

2. International Aid Response

Global support mobilized

China and India have sent experts to assist Nepal’s rescue teams. The UN and Red Cross are coordinating relief efforts, providing satellite imagery, logistics, and medical supplies. Prime Minister Balendra Shah called the flood an “unprecedented and devastating natural disaster,” urging determination in saving lives.

3. Hydropower Workers Trapped

933 workets missing in tunnels

Rescue teams are using controlled blasts and excavators to clear mud and rocks from hydropower tunnels in Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts. Soldiers are working under torchlight, but debris makes access extremely difficult. Families wait anxiously as morgues in Kathmandu reach capacity.
